About me

I am a geospatial professional. I have spent most of my career with Earth observation data from satellites and aircraft; today I work mainly with drone sensors, from RGB cameras to LiDAR. The goal has stayed the same: turning raw data into information people can use. My work sits at the intersection of remote sensing, data engineering and machine learning. I analyze imagery at scale, build pipelines and backends that serve it, and fine-tune foundation models, alongside open-source LLMs and VLMs, to find what matters in it. Since November 2025 I have been doing this at Beagle Systems, with data from long-range drones.

From orbit to drone

My path in remote sensing, from satellites to drones. Scroll to descend.

  1. 01 · Satellite · from 2016

    It started in orbit.

    My work began with satellite data from Landsat 7 and 8, Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, MODIS, ASTER and TRMM: optical, radar and thermal views of the Earth from several hundred kilometers up. At 30 m per pixel, a whole village is a handful of colored squares.

    ~705 km · 30 m pixels

  2. 02 · Aerial · from 2021

    Then closer: aerial imagery.

    Then I moved closer: aerial orthophotos, captured from aircraft about a kilometer above the ground and stitched into seamless, georeferenced mosaics. At 20 cm per pixel, every house, road and tree appears.

    ~1 km · 20 cm pixels

  3. 03 · Drone · since 11/2025

    Today: drones.

    Today I work with data from long-range drones that fly beyond visual line of sight, carrying cameras and LiDAR at low altitude. At about 2 cm per pixel, single objects stand out, and a detection model can find them.

    ~100 m · ~2 cm pixels

Professional experience

  1. 11/2025 – present

    Geospatial Data Specialist Beagle Systems, Hamburg

    Beagle Systems flies long-range drones beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) to monitor pipelines, utilities and other infrastructure.

    • Automatic georeferencing of aerial images with AI-based feature matching.
    • Right-of-way inspection: fine-tuned foundation models for object detection, with open-source VLMs checking the results.
    • Georeferencing and classification of LiDAR data from the company’s drones.
    • Live analysis: heavy-machinery detection on an NVIDIA Jetson, with detections sent directly to the customer.
    • Backend with PostGIS and TiTiler for serving large GeoTIFFs.
    • Drone flight planning: primary and duplicate checks.
