Professional experience

  1. 11/2025 – present

    Geospatial Data Specialist Beagle Systems, Hamburg

    Beagle Systems flies long-range drones beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) to monitor pipelines, utilities and other infrastructure.

    • Automatic georeferencing of aerial images with AI-based feature matching.
    • Right-of-way inspection: fine-tuned foundation models for object detection, with open-source VLMs checking the results.
    • Georeferencing and classification of LiDAR data from the company’s drones.
    • Live analysis: heavy-machinery detection on an NVIDIA Jetson, with detections sent directly to the customer.
    • Backend with PostGIS and TiTiler for serving large GeoTIFFs.
    • Drone flight planning: primary and duplicate checks.

  2. 03/2024 – 10/2025

    Geospatial (EO) Data Scientist, part-time Karuna Technology UG, Germany

    • Differential InSAR pipeline with ISCE: automated acquisition, preprocessing and interferogram generation.
    • Google Earth Engine workflows for Sentinel-1 time series (Refined Lee filter) feeding a soil-moisture ML model.
    • Urban tree-species classification from fused multispectral and SAR data; training data for tree-crown segmentation.
    • Vector data pre- and post-processing in QGIS.

  3. 04/2021 – 02/2024

    GIS & RS Specialist MapTransfer, Germany

    • Spatial data workflows for a construction company: georeferencing, orthophoto mosaics, cloud-optimized GeoTIFFs on AWS, PostGIS–QGIS integration.
    • In-house courses on LiDAR in QGIS and spatial data analysis; course development for Spatial SQL, ALKIS and GeoServer.
    • Geodata management and visualization for a telecom project based on ALKIS.
    • Web GIS: PostGIS vector layers in OpenLayers.
